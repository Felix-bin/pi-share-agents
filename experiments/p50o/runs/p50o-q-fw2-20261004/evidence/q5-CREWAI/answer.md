## Answer

**The city is Nanjing, and its total land area is 2,548 sq mi.**

The chain that fixes it, as the corpus actually supports it:

1. **Greeting → city.** `003-Sino-Tibetan-relations-during-the-Ming-dynasty.md:3` (verbatim): *"During his travels beginning in 1403, Deshin Shekpa was induced by further exhortations by the Ming court to visit Nanjing by April 10, 1407. … The Yongle Emperor came out of the palace in Nanjing to greet the Karmapa and did not require him to kowtow like a tributary vassal."* The emperor is the Yongle Emperor; the person greeted is written as "**the Karmapa**"; the city named in that same passage is **Nanjing**. (The greeting sentence itself carries no personal name; the preceding sentence of the same line supplies "Deshin Shekpa".)

2. **Greeted person named.** `009-…:3` equates that Karmapa with a name and dates: *"On March 10, 1403, the Yongle Emperor invited Deshin Shekpa, 5th Karmapa Lama (1384–1415), to his court…"*; `012-…:3` corroborates: *"the Yongle Emperor (r. 1402–1424) made a concerted effort to build a secular and religious alliance with Deshin Shekpa (1384–1415), the Karmapa of the Karma Kagyu school."* `016-…:3` places the same person in the same city and records the title Yongle gave him: *"At Linggu Temple in Nanjing, he presided over the religious ceremonies… During his stay in Nanjing, Deshin Shekpa was bestowed the title 'Great Treasure Prince of Dharma' by the Yongle Emperor."* `021-…:3` is consistent (Hou Xian and Zhi Guang return to Nanjing in 1407).

3. **Edict → Karmapa.** `002-…:3` (verbatim): *"The Information Office of the State Council of the PRC preserves an edict of the Zhengtong Emperor (r. 1435–1449) addressed to the Karmapa in 1445… Zhengtong had the following message delivered to the Great Treasure Prince of Dharma, the Karmapa:"* The addressee is a **Karmapa** bearing the title "Great Treasure Prince of Dharma" — the title `016:3` says Yongle bestowed on Deshin Shekpa.

4. **City → area.** `036-Nanjing.md:3` (verbatim): *"Nanjing, with a total land area of 6,598 square kilometres (2,548 sq mi), is situated in the heartland of drainage area of lower reaches of Yangtze River…"* This is the **only** statement of Nanjing's area anywhere in the worktree (67 files), and it uses the words "**total land area**". The square-mile figure the source itself states is **2,548 sq mi**.

**Verification status and qualifications that belong with the claims above:**

- **The edict link is a title link, not an identity link — a documented contradiction, not a reading artefact.** `002:3` dates the edict to **1445** under the **Zhengtong Emperor (r. 1435–1449)**, but the greeted individual, Deshin Shekpa, is dated **1384–1415** in `009:3` and `012:3`. He died 30 years before that edict, so the 1445 addressee cannot be the same man the Yongle Emperor greeted; the two documents join only through the shared office/title "*Karmapa / Great Treasure Prince of Dharma*". This does not disturb the answer, because both candidate readings of "the edict" point at the same city: whichever document is meant, the city is fixed by the greeting passage (`003:3`), and `003:3` is the only file that names a city for the greeting.
